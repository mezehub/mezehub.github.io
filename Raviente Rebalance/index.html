Raviente Rebalance

Published: July 14, 2025 | Updated: July 30, 2025

The purpose of this rebalance is to achieve the following:

  • Centralize the main source for Raviente equipment (Decorations, Weapons) away from the Road Shop.

What this means:

  • Divine Power Gems have been adjusted to be much more efficient to farm from Raviente, than Hunting Road.
  • Raviente Decorations are now much more efficient to farm from Raviente, than Hunting Road.

✽ Raviente Decorations and Divine Power Gems are still accessible via the Roadshop, however at an increased price rate.

This update serves as a way to increase options for players and incentivize farming the materials from the original source.

Rebalance Outline

The loot/drop system for Raviente and its rewards have changed.

Here is an outline:

✽ The following items have been added to drop from Berserk & Extreme Raviente:

- Ravi Deco Tkt (Used to exchange for Berserk, Violent, and Normal Raviente Decorations)

- EX Ravi Deco Tkt (Used to exchange for Extreme Raviente Decorations)

- Ravi Transmog Tkt (Used to exchange for Raviente Transmogs)

✽ The following item has been added to the Road Shop (5 RDP) with a restriction of GR999:

- Ravi Combiner Tkt (Used to access Raviente Combiner Exchanges)

This is essential to avoid abuse of the new exchange system before reaching max GR.

Decoration Exchange

After slaying Berserk/Extreme Raviente, you will receive guaranteed drops of either "Ravi Deco Tkt" or "EX Ravi Deco Tkt".

By taking these tickets to the combiner, you can exchange them for decorations.

Navigate to the "Premium Tickets" option at the Combiner NPC.

Ravi Deco Tkt will only drop from Berserk Raviente, and can be used to exchange for all level of Raviente Decorations, however will cost more tickets to exchange for Extreme Raviente Decorations.

EX Ravi Deco Tkt will only drop from Extreme Raviente, and can be used to exchange for all Raviente Decorations at a cheaper exchange rate.

As shown above, it will require a larger amount of Ravi Deco Tickets to exchange for an Extreme Raviente Decoration, compared to other levels.

To utilize the exchange, remember to purchase Ravi Combiner tickets from the Road Shop.

✽ Remember, you can still craft decorations with the material drops from Raviente via refining!

Refer to the Decoration Refinement Guide for detailed information.

Divine Power Gems

As mentioned above, the amount of Divine Power Gems dropped has been substantially increased.

Lesser gems such as Heavy Power Gem & True Power Gem have been removed from the Berserk/Extreme Raviente reward pool.

The Reward pool now instead features fixed Divine Power Gem drops across the hunt.

Here is a breakdown of the drops for Berserk Raviente:

Gems per Phase:

  • Phase 1: 10,000 Divine Power Gems
  • Phase 2: 10,000 Divine Power Gems
  • Phase 3: 10,000 Divine Power Gems
  • Phase 4: 10,000 Divine Power Gems
  • Phase 5: 10,000 Divine Power Gems
  • Carve Phase: 25,000 Divine Power Gems (with bonus chance for more)
  • Total (fixed) per run: 75,000 Divine Power Gems

Killing Berserk Raviente 6 times will yield a guarantee of 450,000 Divine Power Gems, which currently equates to 1,350,000 Road Points.

✽ During the Bi-Weekly Raviente Event, reward skills will be enabled! This means you can bring Lucky Charms onto the Carve Quest for bonus gems.

✽ Extreme Raviente drops double the amount of gems, compared to Berserk Raviente.

Transmogs

With this update comes the ability to transmog Raviente Armor!

First, make sure to have some transmog ticket drops from Raviente.

To view/unlock them, navigate to the Smithy, and go through this option path: [Armors → Transmog → Unlock Transmog → [Armor Type] → Premium]

You will see the newly listed Raviente Transmogs:

Each piece only requires one transmog ticket to unlock.
